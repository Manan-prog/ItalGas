How to read this map

Grey ribbons show where the radar passed. Each drive covers a strip about 1.5 m wide under the van. Click a ribbon for that drive's positioning quality.

Glow shows the chance that a pipe lies under that spot, from 0 (none) to 1 (bright), worked out from the radar of every drive. Pipes crossing the drive are blurred along the street by their position uncertainty, so a poorly placed pipe shows as a wider, dimmer band. Pipes running along the street are added with the chance measured from repeat drives (see "How sure are these?"). Where drives overlap, the highest value is shown. Zoom in for finer cells (0.15 m from zoom 17).

Blue lines are pipes crossing the street. Thick: confident (both radar rows saw it). Thin: seen by one row with chance 0.9 or more.

Green lines are pipes that may run along the street. Solid: confirmed by drives in independent lanes, above chance. Dashed: candidates (one drive, or a repeat that is not above chance). Candidates add nothing to the glow, because their chance of being a pipe has not been measured above zero.

Evenly spaced crossings on bridges or over culverts are probably structure (deck beams, joints, rebar), not pipes.

Positions come from the real-time GPS track, moved 1.19 m forward along the direction of travel to correct an assumed timing bias. They can be out by 0.3 to 1 m or more; each popup gives the measured uncertainty where it exists.

No Italgas network map has been received, so nothing on this map has been checked against the real gas network. Municipal sewer and storm records (Comune di Savona, CC BY 4.0) are shown for comparison only.

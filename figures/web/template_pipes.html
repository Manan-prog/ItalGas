How to read this map

Ribbons show where the radar passed (about 1.4 m wide under the van). Grey: the street was driven once. Blue-grey: another drive passed in a neighbouring lane (0.5 to 1.2 m away) for at least 50 m. Click a ribbon for the drive's details.

Raster (red to green): for every 0.5 m along the drive and every 4 cm across it, P is the share of cells with that much evidence of a compact reflector that the same data with the antenna order shuffled does not produce. The strongest depth is shown; where drives overlap, the highest value. The top 0.33 m and the outer 0.2 m on each side of the array are not evaluated.

Green lines are drawn from the raster by one rule (see Checks). All lines are drawn alike; hover to see whether a drive in another lane repeats it, covers it without repeating it, or does not cover it, and click for details. The few lines that a second lane repeats carry a white core.

Blue lines are pipes crossing the street, from a separate detector run on a subset of drives. Thick: seen by both radar rows. Thin: one row with P 0.9 or more.

Positions come from the real-time GPS track, moved 1.19 m forward along the direction of travel (an assumed timing bias). They can be out by 0.3 to 1 m or more; popups give the measured uncertainty where it exists.

Radar only, provisional. No Italgas network map has been received, so nothing here has been checked against the real gas network. Municipal sewer and storm records (Comune di Savona, CC BY 4.0) are shown for comparison only.

Two genuinely useful public sources exist for Savona: (A) the Comune di Savona g3w-suite WMS, which serves a 2016 public-lighting point census (4,657 poles), sewer pipes with depth attributes (3,217 foul + 9,061 storm segments) and sewer manholes; and (B) the Regione Liguria WFS (Fees/AccessConstraints "NONE", CC BY 4.0 on the open-data portal) with the CTR 1:5000 2007-2013 "Reti tecnologiche" classes (gas, water, electricity, oil lines), a 2023 "Rete Fognaria" layer and the SNAM transmission gasdotti. No Italgas distribution-network geodata is public anywhere; SINFI is registration-only. Everything below was fetched and tested today (2 Nov 2026) unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Italgas / 2i Rete Gas distribution network: NOTHING PUBLIC
- No open data, shapefile, WMS or map. Italgas only publishes a prose "3D Asset Mapping / digital twin" project page (https://www.italgas.it/progetti/panoramica-progetti/soluzioni/3d-asset-mapping/), no downloads.
- Cartography is released only case-by-case through the MyItalgas portal, section "Coordinamento Sottoservizi" (nulla osta for excavations): https://www.italgas.it/clienti/myitalgas/ (login https://clienti.italgas.it/clienti/login.action; registration as Business/PA/Costruttore etc.). Italgas itself states its planimetrie are "indicative"; a third-party MIT conferenza-di-servizi document shows Italgas sending cartography "in via informale" after a request (https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/conferenza-servizio/2024-07/718_PARERI%20ACQUISITI%20(1).pdf). This is the realistic route: a formal data request to Italgas Reti (Savona area is split: Italgas Reti ~90-95 % of ambito "Savona 2 Nord-Est"; 2i Rete Gas majority in "Savona 1 Sud-Ovest", per AGCM provvedimento 31413, older document — 2i Rete Gas has since been acquired by Italgas per Wikipedia, unverified).
- Only an INSPIRE record (Valle d'Aosta, 2010) exists for a "Metanodotto rete principale" and it is restricted to PA use.

## 6. Regulation: where maps are supposed to be
- DPCM/Direttiva 3 marzo 1999 ("Direttiva Micheli", G.U. 58 of 11 Mar 1999): every municipality must hold georeferenced cartography of network routes and institute a "catasto del sottosuolo" and an Ufficio del Sottosuolo collecting network maps from operators; provincial capitals (>30,000 inhabitants, so Savona) must draft a PUGSS. Text: https://www.bosettiegatti.eu/info/circolari/statali/1999_sottosuolo.htm. Hence the Comune di Savona (Settore 4 / Ufficio del Sottosuolo) is the legal holder of operator maps – that is the formal request target besides Italgas.
- D.Lgs. 15 Feb 2016 n. 33 (art. 4, text https://www.bosettiegatti.eu/INFO/NORME/STATALI/2016_0033.htm) created SINFI; gestori di infrastrutture fisiche incl. gas, public lighting, water "comprese le fognature" must supply ubicazione/tracciato, tipo, uso, punto di contatto; access right is for "operatori di rete" with an interest, on telematic request, and PAs; citizens/researchers are not named; data "in formato aperto e interoperabile" but behind registration. Access procedures amended by DM 26 Mar 2025 (MIMIT page https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/comunicazioni/servizi-alle-imprese/sinfi).
- SINFI portal https://sinfi.it/portal/ (Infratel Italia): no public viewer, no WMS/WFS, access via https://sinfi.it/portal/consultazione-dati/richiesta-di-accesso/ and gateway https://sinfi.it/sinfi_gateway; "first interoperable services" via PDND for accredited bodies. Contacts info@sinfi.it, +39 06 97749311. The data model is public: "Specifiche di contenuto ... Reti di sottoservizi e SINFI" v4.0, 14 Jul 2026, CC BY 4.0, 161 pp, https://geodati.gov.it/geoportale/images/Specifica-SINFI_ver.4.0-2026-07-14.pdf (downloaded to scratchpad/dl/docs/sinfi_spec4.pdf; defines classes for rete di distribuzione del gas, nodi, pozzetti; no numeric positional-accuracy requirement found by grep) plus linee guida v1.2 and loading manual at https://sinfi.it/portal/formato-dati/documentazione/.
- Liguria has no regional sottosuolo cadastre law/WebGIS (unlike Lombardy); water in Savona city is IRETI (ex Acquedotto di Savona, Iren group), sewer/depuration by Consorzio Depurazione Savonese since 2017 (https://www.depuratore.sv.it/sii-servizio-idrico-integrato); neither publishes a WebGIS.

## Bottom line for the project
- Best available "ground truth" proxies now, all free: Savona SIT public-lighting poles (4,657 pts, 2016) and sewer pipes/manholes with material, diameter and depth (12,278 segments); Regione Liguria sewer collectors (2023), SNAM transmission lines, CTR water conduits. All are derived GIS records of unstated accuracy (CTR nominal 1:5000 ≈ ±1 m; municipal sewer DB accuracy unknown); none is a gas distribution map.
- Gas distribution geometry exists only inside Italgas Reti (MyItalgas "Coordinamento Sottoservizi" request) and, by law, at the Comune's Ufficio del Sottosuolo and in SINFI (registered operators/PAs). A written request to Italgas Reti and to Comune di Savona Settore 4 is the only path; label all accuracy claims provisional until then (CLAUDE.md §2).

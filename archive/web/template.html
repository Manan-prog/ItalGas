How to read this map

Grey ribbons show where the radar passed. Each drive covers a strip about 1.5 m wide under the van.

Glow shows the chance that a pipe crosses under that spot, from 0 (none) to 1 (bright). It comes from pipes that cross the drive. Each one is blurred along the street by its position uncertainty, so a poorly placed pipe shows as a wider, dimmer band. Where drives overlap, the highest value is shown.

Blue lines are pipes crossing the street. Thick lines were seen by two or more drives, or by both radar rows (600 and 200 MHz): these are confident. Thin lines were seen once, with chance 0.9 or more.

Green dashed lines are pipes that may run along the street. None has passed the full confidence test yet, so they are candidates and are not in the glow. Click one to see the evidence.

Evenly spaced crossings on bridges or over culverts are probably structure (deck beams, joints, rebar), not pipes.

Click any line for its depth and position uncertainty. Positions can be out by 0.3 to 1 m or more. Single drives are moved 1.19 m forward along their direction of travel to correct an assumed timing bias.

No Italgas network map has been received, so nothing on this map has been checked against the real gas network. Municipal sewer and storm records (Comune di Savona, CC BY 4.0) are shown for comparison only.
